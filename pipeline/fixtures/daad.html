DAAD EPOS Development-Related Postgraduate Courses

The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) funds development-related postgraduate courses at German universities.

2028/29 intake

Applications open on 1 November 2027.

Applications close on 15 January 2028.

Apply at https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/.

What DAAD covers

  • A monthly stipend of 1200 EUR.
  • Travel allowance and health insurance.

Admission results are announced in April 2028.
