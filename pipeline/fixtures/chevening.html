Chevening Scholarships in Pakistan

Chevening is the UK government's international awards programme. It funds one-year master's degrees at any UK university.

2027/28 intake

Applications open on 1 August 2027.

Applications close on 3 November 2027.

Apply at https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/apply/ before the deadline.

What you need

  • A completed undergraduate degree.
  • Two years of work experience.
  • Two reference letters.

How to apply

  1. Create an account on the Chevening portal.
  2. Choose up to four UK master's courses.
  3. Answer the long-form questions and submit.

Interviews are held between February and April 2028.
